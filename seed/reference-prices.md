## New York (Manhattan)
- Hotel $280 / $400 / $600 per room-night (S, engine.com October average $429). 3 rooms (rollaways rarely allowed).
- NYC hotel tax 14.75% + $3.50 per room-night (V, nyc.gov); destination fee up to ~$45 must be in the shown price since Feb 2026.
- Airbnb-style apartments effectively banned for under 30 nights (Local Law 18).
- Observation deck $38–60; Statue of Liberty ferry ~$26; CityPASS $129–164; Broadway average paid $131 (under-4s not admitted); museums $0–37.
- Subway OMNY $3/ride, capped $35/week. Airport ↔ Manhattan for the family (2 vehicles) $170–350. Food $50 / $85 / $130 per person per day. Tips 18–22%.

- Infant car seat: bring your own (checks free) or $70–98 per rental.
